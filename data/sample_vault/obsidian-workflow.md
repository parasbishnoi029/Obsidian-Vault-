# My Obsidian Workflow

How I organise notes day to day.

## Daily notes
One note per day, dated YYYY-MM-DD. Quick capture, no formatting
pressure - the goal is just getting thoughts down.

## Permanent notes
Once an idea from a daily note seems useful long-term, I rewrite it
as its own atomic note - one idea per file, linked to related notes
with [[wiki links]].

## Tags vs links
I mostly use tags (#project, #idea, #reading) for broad categories and
links for direct conceptual relationships between two specific notes.

## Why I'm building a RAG assistant over this vault
Searching Obsidian's built-in search is keyword-only. A RAG assistant
lets me ask "what did I write about X" in natural language and get an
answer that pulls from multiple notes at once instead of a list of
files I have to open myself.
